# A comparison is always exactly two Career Options

The User compares two careers at a time, never three or a ranked list, even
though a User "interested in many things" might want more.

Two is what makes the mechanism work. A forced comparison produces sharper
answers than a ranking, and the Direction ranking in
`0003-rank-by-what-separates-the-options.md` is defined as *what separates the
options* — which is only meaningful with a pair. Widening to N options would
turn that into a scoring exercise, which is the career-fit score `AGENTS.md`
principle 1 forbids.

This is a decision about the size of a *comparison*, not about the size of the
career catalogue. How many Career Options the product offers to choose
between, and what makes a new one cheap or expensive to author, remains open.
