# Direction ranks by what separates the two Career Options

When several Unknowns could be explored next, we order them by how much
exploring one would distinguish the two Career Options under comparison, and
break ties by what the User can realistically arrange next term.

The obvious alternative — rank by what the User most enjoyed, or by the
biggest hole in their evidence — was rejected. An Unknown that both Career
Options treat as important tells the User nothing about the choice in front
of them, however curious they are about it, and an activity they already have
heavy evidence for is not where the remaining uncertainty lives.
`AGENTS.md` principle 7 is to reduce *decision-relevant* uncertainty, and this
is what makes it operational: the highest-value next step is the one whose
outcome most changes which way the User leans.

Consequence: the ranking is only computable against exactly two Career
Options. See `0004-pairwise-comparison.md`.
